## Question

How do we structure Kafka topics and partitioning for message events?

## Decision

- Single topic: `message-created`
- Partition key: `conversationId` — ensures message ordering within a conversation
- Single consumer group for the Elasticsearch indexer service

## Alternatives Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Topic per tenant | Tenant isolation | Topic explosion, operational burden. IaC become mandatory for dedicated infra provisioning |
| Partition by tenantId | Tenant-level ordering | No conversation ordering guarantee |
| **Single topic, partition by conversationId** | Conversation ordering, simple topology | Hot partitions if one conversation is very active |

## Rationale

Message ordering within a conversation is the most valuable ordering guarantee. Single topic keeps Kafka topology simple. Hot partition risk is acceptable at current scale.

## Invalidation Trigger

If a single conversation generates enough volume to bottleneck a partition.
